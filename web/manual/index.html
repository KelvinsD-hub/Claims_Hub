Getting started

Accounts are by invitation only. There is no sign-up page.

  1. An admin invites you. You get a link by email or WhatsApp. It works once and lasts 7 days.
  2. Open the link. Choose Continue with Google with the invited address, or choose a password.
  3. You are in, with the role the admin chose. Your role decides which pages appear in the sidebar on the left.

After that, sign in at claimshub.online with Google or your email and password. Forgot your password? Click Forgot password? on the sign-in page and we email you a reset link. If you joined with Google, you have no Claims Hub password: use Google.

To change your name, password or the light/dark theme, go to Settings. If you are stuck, use Support.

Who sees what

Each person only sees the pages their job needs. This keeps anyone from sending an airline letter or working a legal file by mistake. If you open a page you are not allowed to use, it says "This page is not available for your role." Ask an admin if you need access.

"Admin" here covers both Admin and Super Admin. Some actions are also limited by role, wherever they are on screen:

  • Managers and above send demand letters, give work to someone else, mark a claim Paid, and reopen anything that was closed.
  • The legal team (Solicitors, and Managers and above) send the final notice and move a claim between legal stages.
  • Everyone can take unassigned work, add notes, and make the normal stage moves.

Your day in Claims Hub

Every lead and claim has three things that tell you what to do:

Owner
The person handling it. Claims with the legal team also have a lawyer.
Next action
What the record is waiting for, such as "Chase the client for evidence".
Due date
When that action is due. Past the date, it shows as overdue in red.

The next action and due date are set for you each time the stage changes. You do not have to work them out.

A good routine

  1. Open the Dashboard for today's numbers: new leads, open claims, overdue work and claims won.
  2. Go to Leads or Claims and click the Overdue chip. Clear those first.
  3. Click Mine to see only your own work, and go through it by due date.
  4. Pick up anything new and unassigned with Take.

Click any row to open its file. The file shows the client, the flight, documents, the money, the action buttons, and on the right the History: every stage change, note and letter, with who did it and when.

The pipeline, step by step

1. A lead arrives from the website

Who
The client, on claimsassistltd.com
Shows in Claims Hub as
New lead on the Leads page
You have
1 day to contact them

The client checks their flight on the website, answers a few questions about what happened, and gives their contact details. Many clients also sign the Letter of Authority there and then, and give their date of birth, address, booking reference and bank details.

Open the lead to see everything they gave:

  • Contact: email, phone, how they prefer to be contacted, and Came in through: the ad link or partner that brought them (see Links). A lead sent by a referral partner also shows Referred by.
  • What happened: airline, flight number and date, delay, ticket price, passengers and booking reference.
  • Signed authority, if they signed online: the name they signed with, when, date of birth, address, anyone else on the booking, and the signature itself.

As soon as they submit:

  • A new lead appears at the top of Leads, with no owner. It sits in the shared queue until someone takes it.
  • The client gets an email confirming we have their claim, with a reference number.

Staff can add a lead by hand with Add a lead, for someone who phoned or walked in. Do not send clients any other form link: ads and posts use the links on the Links page.

The 14-day cancellation periodThe client can choose on the website to let us start straight away. If they did not, nothing may be sent to the airline until 14 days after they signed. Claims Hub enforces this itself and shows the date the hold ends.

2. Work the lead

Who
Agents and Managers
Stages
New lead → Contacted → Qualified or Rejected
You have
1 day to contact, then 3 days to decide
  1. Open the lead and click Take so everyone knows it is yours.
  2. Contact the client by phone or email. Check the flight, the delay or cancellation, and what they told us.
  3. Click Change stage and move it to Contacted. Add a note of what you learned with Add note.
  4. Decide:
    • It is a claim we can take: move it to Qualified. This opens the claim automatically, owned by you, with everything the client already gave: flight number and date, route, booking reference, delay, signature, address and any files they sent.
    • It is not: move it to Rejected. You must give a reason.

Once a lead is qualified, the work continues on the claim. Use Open the claim in the lead file to go straight to it.

Something missing before you can decide, such as the flight number or a boarding pass? Use Request information rather than retyping it from a phone call.

Not sure? Open the AI assistant tab in the lead and run a lead triage. It gives a second opinion; the decision is still yours.

3. The client sends evidence and signs

Who
The client; the owner chases
Stages
Details Pending → Terms Pending → Ready For Review
You have
3 days to chase evidence, then 2 days to chase the signature

When the claim opens, the client is emailed automatically. What they get depends on whether they signed on the website:

  • They signed online: an email saying their claim is open and there is nothing they need to do. We already have their details and signature.
  • They did not: an email with a private link to the evidence form. They fill in their flight details, what happened, ID, bank details and photos of their documents, then sign the terms. The claim moves to Terms Pending and then Ready For Review by itself.

If one or two things are still missing, do not send the whole form again: use Request information and tick just those.

Your job here is to chase. If the due date passes, call or email the client and record what you did with Add note. If they no longer want to go ahead, move the claim to Withdrawn with a reason.

4. Review the claim

Who
The owner, or any agent
Stages
Ready For Review → Under Review → Demand Pending
You have
1 day to start, 2 days to finish
  1. Move the claim to Under Review. The client is emailed that we are reviewing their claim.
  2. Check the documents in the claim file, or in the Evidence Locker. Make sure names, flight number, date and route match the booking.
  3. Use the Part 19 calculator to work out what the client is owed under the Nigerian rules.
  4. Fix any mistakes with Edit details.
  5. When everything is right, move the claim to Demand Pending. If something is missing, move it back to Details Pending and tell the client what you need.

The AI assistant can write a case brief or check a document for you. Read what it says, then check it yourself.

5. Send the demand letter

Who
Managers and above
Stages
Demand Pending → Awaiting Reply
You have
1 day to send

The Demand letters page lists every claim waiting for its letter, with what is ready and what is missing.

  1. Pick a claim. A claim cannot be sent until it has the client's name, airline, flight number, flight date, route and signature. Anything missing is listed in red.
  2. Check the warnings too: the amount, the booking reference, and the delay or reason. These do not block the letter, but a letter without them is weaker.
  3. Check the airline's email address. Claims Hub suggests one; change it if you know a better one.
  4. Click Send demand letter. The letter goes with the client's Letter of Authority attached.

The claim moves to Awaiting Reply by itself, and the client is emailed that we have contacted the airline. The airline has 14 days to reply.

If it will not send"Held until" means the client's cancellation period has not ended; it sends after that date. "Send failed" means the email did not go; check the address and try again.

A manager can resend the demand letter from the claim file if it needs to go again.

7. The legal team takes over

Who
Solicitors, and Managers and above
Stage
With Solicitor
Where
The Legal Workspace

Every claim with the legal team is listed in the Legal Workspace. The client is emailed that their claim has gone to our legal team. A lawyer takes the claim, or a manager assigns one.

Inside the legal team, the claim moves through legal stages. This is the usual route; a lawyer can move between them as the case needs:

Legal stageWhat it meansNext action due
Legal reviewA lawyer is reading the file.2 days to review and send the final notice
Final notice sentThe final legal notice has gone to the airline.7 days for the airline to pay
NCAA complaint filedA complaint is with the NCAA consumer protection directorate.Check on it every 14 days
Court proceedingsDecided claim by claim, never automatic. You must record why.An update every 30 days

Send the final notice with Send final notice in the claim file. When the case ends, move the claim to Won or Lost. If the airline starts talking again, it can go back to Awaiting Reply.

8. Close the claim and pay the client

Who
The owner; a manager marks it Paid
Stages
Won → Paid
You have
7 days to pay the client
  • Won: the client is emailed the good news. Pay them their share after our fee, then a manager moves the claim to Paid.
  • Lost: the client is emailed. A lost claim can still go to the legal team later.
  • Withdrawn: the client cancelled, or we stopped. A reason is required.

Only a manager can reopen a closed claim.

Tools

Ask the client for more information

Who
Anyone working the lead or claim
Where
Request information in the lead or claim file
The link lasts
14 days, and works once
  1. Open the lead or claim and click Request information.
  2. Tick what is missing: flight number, flight date, booking reference, ticket price, date of birth, address, phone, boarding pass, ticket or receipt, photo ID, a message from the airline, or expense receipts. The ones with a paperclip are files.
  3. Add up to three questions of your own if you need to, and a short note to the client.
  4. Click Send request. The client is emailed a link to a short form on claimsassistltd.com that asks only for what you ticked. You also see the link, with Copy link, to send by WhatsApp.

The next action becomes Chase the client for the information asked for, due in 5 days.

When the client answers, everything goes straight onto the record: typed answers into the right fields, and files into the record's documents. The next action becomes Review the information the client sent, due today, and whoever owns the record gets an email. The client can also say they do not have something; you see that too.

Each request is listed in the file under Requests to the client: what was asked, what came back, and the files to open. Use Withdraw on an open request to stop its link working. Sending a new request on the same record replaces the old one.

Bank detailsBank details cannot be requested this way. Collect them through the evidence form or by phone.

Links for ads and posts

Who
Managers and admins
Where
The Links page
A link looks like
claimsassistltd.com/go/fb

Every ad, post or message that sends people to the website should use one of these links, so we know which ones bring in claims.

  1. The first time, click Make a link for each social network to get one each for Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok and WhatsApp.
  2. For a particular campaign, click New link: choose where it will be used, give it a name you will recognise (for example "Facebook October delays ad"), check the link, and choose whether it opens the home page or goes straight to the claim check. The link cannot be changed later.
  3. Click the copy icon next to a link and paste it into the ad or post.

For each link the page shows clicks (and when it was last clicked), leads (and what share of clicks became leads), signed authorities, claims opened, claims won and money recovered, with totals underneath. Leads that came another way, such as people who found the site themselves, are listed at the bottom.

  • A click is counted only when a person opens the link, not when Facebook, WhatsApp or X fetches it to draw a preview.
  • Someone who clicks a link and claims within 30 days, from any page of the site, is credited to that link.
  • Turning a link off with its switch sends visitors to the home page and credits nobody. Links are never deleted, so past figures stay.

Referral partners

Who manages them
Admins
Where
The Referral partners page
Their own page
claimsassistltd.com/partner

A referral partner is someone outside Claims Assist who sends us passengers, such as a travel agent. They are not staff and never sign in to Claims Hub.

  1. Click Invite a partner and enter their name, email and phone. They get their own link (for example claimsassistltd.com/go/p-tunde-bakare) and an email with a button that signs them in to their partner page. No password.
  2. After inviting, you see both links with copy buttons: their referral link for passengers, and their sign-in link for them only. Send them by WhatsApp if the email does not arrive.
  3. The partner shares their referral link. Every passenger who claims through it is credited to them. A partner's link wins over an earlier ad, so the person who personally sent the client gets the credit.

On their partner page, a partner sees their link with copy and WhatsApp buttons, their totals, and each person they referred: first name and initial only, the airline and route, and how far the claim has got. They never see contact details, documents, bank details, notes or amounts, and they cannot change anything. They are emailed when someone new comes through their link and each time that person's claim moves on.

On the Referral partners page you see each partner's clicks, referrals, signed, claims and won, and whether they have signed in yet. From the ⋮ menu: Send a sign-in link if theirs expired, or Suspend, which closes their page and stops new clients being credited to them. Past referrals stay credited, and Restore undoes it.

What partners must not doFill in or sign a claim for someone else, collect passengers' documents, ID or bank details, promise an amount or a result, or contact an airline about a claim. Each passenger fills in and signs their own claim through the partner's link. Tell an admin if a partner breaks these rules.

The AI assistant

The AI assistant tab in a lead or claim file can do four things: triage a lead, write a case brief, read an airline reply, and check a client's document.

  • It only advises. It never changes a record, sends anything, or works out the amount owed.
  • Always check what it says before you act on it.
  • It is never sent bank details, ID numbers, emails, phone numbers or signatures.
If a client does not want AI usedClick The client does not want AI used in the AI assistant tab. The assistant then stops working on that lead and its claim. Only a manager can turn it back on.

Rules to remember

  • Stages only move forward in the order shown. If a move is not allowed, Claims Hub tells you which moves are.
  • Everything is recorded. Stage changes, notes, letters and assignments all go into the History with your name and the time.
  • Rejecting a lead, withdrawing a claim and going to court all need a reason.
  • Nothing goes to the airline inside the client's cancellation period.
  • Never promise a client that we will pay court fees.
  • Take before you work. If a record already belongs to someone else, ask them or a manager.
  • Which link or partner brought a lead cannot be changed. The website and the server set it, so the figures on Links and Referral partners can be trusted.

For admins

  • Staff: click Invite staff, enter the person's name, email and role, and send the link by email, WhatsApp, or both. Give each person the lowest role their job needs. Only a Super Admin can invite another Super Admin.
  • Invites not yet used are listed under Waiting to join. Use New link if theirs expired or got lost (the old link stops working), or Cancel to withdraw it.
  • Change someone's role, or remove their access, in the staff list.
  • Monitor: the health of the whole pipeline, overdue work, each person's workload, recent activity and AI use.
  • Referral partners: invite, suspend and restore partners, and send them a new sign-in link. See Referral partners.
